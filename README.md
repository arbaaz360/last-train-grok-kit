# Last Train — kit for Grok Imagine

A one-minute anime short: a girl says goodbye to her late grandmother across a level crossing as a train passes.
16 keyframes (`keyframes/s01.jpg` … `s16.jpg`, 1920x1080) and one motion prompt per shot in `PROMPTS.md`;
the full storyboard with durations and the one spoken line is `storyboard.md`.

For each shot: image-to-video from its keyframe with its prompt, 16:9, shortest length, sound on. Keep the
hand-drawn anime look; nothing that is not in the keyframe may appear. Name each clip `<shot>.mp4`.
Hero shots first: s10, s08, s12, s02, s09, s14, s16, s05, s11, s13.
