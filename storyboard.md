# LAST TRAIN — storyboard (≈60 s, 16:9, 1920x1080, English)

A rural Japanese level crossing at dusk after rain. Mio (17) comes to cross with a paper bag of her grandmother's things.
On the far side her grandmother Haru, who died in spring, is waiting the way she always did. The train passes between
them; Mio shouts her goodbye over it; when the last carriage clears, the far side is empty. The barrier lifts. She crosses.

Look: a modern Japanese animated film. Hyper-detailed painted backgrounds, towering cumulus lit orange from below, wet
asphalt and puddles mirroring the sky, lens bloom on every light, cel-shaded characters with soft gradient shading and
fine line work, animated on 2s and 3s (held frames, no floaty motion). Cicadas, a crossing bell, one train.

## People
- **Mio**, 17. Shoulder-length black hair with a loose strand that falls over her right eye, dark eyes, small mouth.
  Summer school uniform: white short-sleeved sailor blouse with a navy collar and red neck ribbon, navy pleated skirt,
  white sneakers, a thin grey cardigan tied at her waist. Carries a brown paper bag with both hands.
- **Haru**, 82. Small, slightly stooped, grey hair in a loose bun, kind lined face, a soft closed-mouth smile. Pale blue
  housecoat with a tiny white floral print, over a cream blouse; house slippers. Hands folded in front of her.

## Place
A single-track level crossing on a country road in Japan: wooden barrier posts with striped arms, a signal with two red
lamps and a bell, a small concrete platform and shelter beyond, rice fields and low green hills, telephone poles.
Dusk after rain, late summer: wet asphalt, puddles, cumulus lit orange from below, a thin band of blue-grey at the horizon.
